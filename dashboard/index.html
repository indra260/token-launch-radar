How the ranking works

Each protocol with no token yet is priced off the live, tokened peers in its own category: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of those peers, capped at the 90th percentile and shrunk toward the global median, times the protocol's TVL, times small adjustments for its seven-day momentum, how long it has been tracked, and how many chains its TVL spans. The farm plan under each protocol is written from its own record — its chains, its size and its recent move — and nothing here promises a token will ever launch.
